Motivation

Do we still need flow-driven tracking when stable geometry is already available?

FPGS reformulates the coupled tracking-and-mapping pipeline as a fixed-geometry Gaussian mapping backend.

External camera poses and intrinsics are estimated upstream and kept fixed during reconstruction. A first-frame depth prior initializes the Gaussian scene; all subsequent mapping is driven only by RGB photometric supervision.

Core design
  • No online pose refinement
  • No optical-flow or projection-flow loss
  • No persistent depth supervision
  • Only Gaussian scene parameters are optimized
Method

Flow-free, fixed-geometry Gaussian mapping

Tracking and mapping are systematically decoupled. The reconstruction backend receives fixed poses, fixed intrinsics, RGB frames, and a first-frame depth prior.

01

External geometry

Camera poses and intrinsics are obtained from an upstream geometry module and remain fixed.

02

Initialization

The first-frame depth prior is back-projected to initialize the Gaussian scene.

03

RGB-only mapping

Each frame updates Gaussian position, scale, rotation, opacity, and appearance through photometric loss.

04

Global refinement

Sampled views refine the final scene without reintroducing tracking, flow, or depth supervision.
